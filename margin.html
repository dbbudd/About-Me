Margin

Your annotations. Your voice. Your judgement.

Feedback that starts with you

Margin helps educators work through student writing using Apple Pencil marks, spoken observations and a rubric. On-device AI helps turn that work into feedback you can review and edit.

The teacher stays in control of the final judgement. Margin is designed to support assessment, not automate it.

What Margin does

Mark naturally

Read and annotate writing with Apple Pencil, keeping your observations connected to the work.

Speak your feedback

Capture spoken commentary while marking, with on-device transcription instead of storing recordings.

Review and refine

Use rubric context and AI-assisted drafts to develop feedback, then make the final decisions yourself.

Getting started

  1. Create a class and assignment in Margin.
  2. Import a supported Word document (.docx) to review.
  3. Mark up the writing with Apple Pencil and, if you choose, use the microphone to capture commentary.
  4. Review suggested feedback and rubric judgements before exporting a report.

Margin is designed for iPad. AI-powered features require a compatible Apple Intelligence device; manual marking remains available when AI is unavailable.

Frequently asked questions

Does Margin send student writing to an AI service?

No. Margin's AI features use Apple's on-device Foundation Models. It does not send student work to a developer-operated server or an external AI provider for feedback generation.

Can I use Margin without Apple Intelligence?

You can still open and mark work manually. AI-assisted feedback requires Apple Intelligence to be available and enabled on a compatible iPad.

Does Margin save my spoken audio?

No. Voice commentary is transcribed on the device; the audio recording itself is not stored. Text observations can be saved with your marking.

Where are student documents and annotations stored?

Margin stores converted document content, annotations and feedback locally on your device. Original documents remain in the file locations you chose, which may be managed by a separate file provider.
