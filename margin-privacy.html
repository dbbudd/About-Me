Margin · Privacy by design

Privacy Policy

Student work stays under your control.

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Margin is an iPad app for educators to mark student writing. It is designed to process writing, annotations and AI-assisted feedback on your device rather than on a server operated by the developer.

Information collected by the developer

Margin does not use developer-operated accounts, advertising, third-party analytics or tracking. The developer does not collect student submissions, annotations, rubric assessments, feedback or voice recordings through the app. If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to send by email.

Documents and local storage

Margin stores converted text from imported documents, annotations, feedback, rubrics and related assessment records locally on your iPad so that markings remain attached to the correct text. Deleting a submission from the app removes its associated app records. The original imported file may remain in its original location, independently of Margin.

Documents opened from Files, Google Drive, OneDrive or another storage provider are also subject to that provider's services and privacy terms. Choosing to export or share a report sends it to a destination you select; Margin does not control that destination.

On-device AI

Margin uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models for AI-assisted feedback and related assistance. Student writing and grading context are processed locally on the iPad, not transmitted to external AI providers or developer servers for inference. AI output is a suggestion: the educator reviews and controls what is accepted or exported. Features requiring Apple Intelligence may be unavailable on an unsupported device.

Microphone and speech

If you enable spoken commentary, Margin uses the microphone and on-device transcription to capture your observations while you mark. The audio itself is not saved; transcribed comments can be retained with the assessment. You can choose not to use the microphone.

Backups and device access

Local app data may be included in iPad backups according to the operating system and your backup settings. Apple and any storage provider you choose operate under their own privacy policies. Protect access to your iPad, especially when handling identifiable student information.

Children's privacy

Margin is intended for educators. It does not offer student accounts or collect children's personal information for the developer. Educators are responsible for handling any student work they import or export in line with their school's requirements.

Changes to this policy

If how Margin handles data changes, this policy will be updated and the date above revised.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact dbbudd [at] gmail.com. Please don't send identifiable student work in a support email.
